n8n · Power Automate · AI · Spring Boot · Angular

AI-powered omnichannel complaint management

A multi-location consumer brand received complaints in eight places: a web form, social media, CRM surveys, QR forms in stores, three shared mailboxes, Google reviews, the phone and paper. I designed one platform that brings them all into a single ticket inbox.

  • n8n captures each channel through webhooks, an IMAP listener and a reviews poller that runs every 15 minutes.
  • An AI step decides whether a message is a complaint and assigns a category. Above 80% confidence, the ticket is routed automatically.
  • Spring Boot handles tickets, SLAs, JWT roles and real-time alerts; Angular shows KPIs and exports PDF and Excel reports.
  • Replies go back through n8n to the same channel the customer used.

My role: solutions architect, process analysis and integration design.

Shopify · Cloudflare Workers · WhatsApp · HubSpot

Automatic invoicing and WhatsApp delivery for a Shopify store

Every paid order had to produce a legally valid electronic invoice. I designed an integration that handles it from payment to the customer's phone, with no one touching it.

  • Shopify's order/paid webhook triggers a small serverless Cloudflare Worker that maps the order and calls the e-invoicing API.
  • The provider signs the invoice, gets it authorized by the tax authority and returns the PDF.
  • ManyChat sends the invoice on WhatsApp, and HubSpot keeps the contact for CRM and email marketing.

My role: integration architecture and serverless middleware.

BPMN 2.0 · Spring Boot · Spring Security · Workflow

Digital incident reporting, from BPMN model to Spring Boot

A company with several sites tracked safety incidents on paper. I modeled the whole process in BPMN 2.0, from detection and severity assessment to evidence, notifications and legal review, and turned it into a layered Spring Boot architecture.

  • Staff fill in a digital form and upload photos, videos and documents.
  • Alerts go out automatically, to different people depending on severity.
  • Overdue cases escalate on their own, and reports are generated on a schedule.
  • Spring Security with JWT, OAuth2 and role-based access.

My role: business process modeling and software architecture.

Google Cloud · Docker · Next.js · Supabase · PWA

Cloud deployment for a multi-site web and Android platform

I designed and documented the production deployment of a platform used across several sites, both as an installable web app and as an Android app.

  • One Google Cloud VM runs everything in Docker, with Caddy as the only public entry point and automatic HTTPS.
  • Next.js serves the interface and API; self-hosted Supabase provides auth, storage and realtime.
  • PostgreSQL row-level security keeps each site's data separate, and scheduled jobs escalate unresolved issues.
  • Push notifications through Firebase (Android) and Web Push (browser).

My role: cloud architecture, deployment and handover documentation.
